## Full description (~200 words)

Use this as the opening block of the README, or as the project summary on a
portfolio page.

---

Lumina Magnifier is a Chrome extension (Manifest V3) that puts a magnifying lens
over any web page or photo — and then helps you actually read what's under it.

Fine print, receipts, medication leaflets and product labels are the target. The
lens follows your pointer or the text caret, zooms from 100% to 1000%, and takes
any shape and size you type — including a tall strip for a column of small print.
It renders from a single screenshot, so panning and zooming stay smooth, and
nothing ever leaves your machine.

Version 1.3.0 makes it a reading aid rather than just a lens. A reading guide dims
the page to one band so your eye cannot drift to the wrong line. Bold text thickens
faint strokes the way professional low-vision software does. A reading strip
reflows the text under the lens into legible prose, on a theme you choose. And
read-aloud speaks it sentence by sentence, highlighting each one as it goes.

There are no runtime dependencies, no build step, no network calls and no
analytics — everything runs locally in the browser.

It is verified by 192 assertions across three suites that drive real headless
Chrome over the DevTools Protocol: no test framework, no `npm install`.
